Goal
----
NVIDIA Omniverse/Kit app (SimulCrowd) that loads a venue (GLB→USD),
runs intent-driven agents on a NavMesh, demonstrates fire
evacuation from perception, and later swaps rule-based decisions for
batched SLM inference.
